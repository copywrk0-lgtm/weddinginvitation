TANISHKA & YASH — CINEMATIC WEDDING INVITATION

HOW TO OPEN
  Unzip the folder, then open index.html in Chrome or Safari.
  Works without installation, a server, or npm packages.
  The animated video and optional soundtrack are INCLUDED in /assets.
  Google web fonts require internet; elegant fallback fonts are included in CSS.

CUSTOMIZE DATE & VENUE
  Open script.js with any text editor and edit the first lines:
    date: '24 November 2027',
    venue: 'Your venue, Your city',
  Leave blank to show "To be announced".
  Please do not publish until the wedding details are confirmed.

PERSONAL CINEMATIC FOOTAGE
  Replace assets/cinematic-loop.mp4 with a video of your choice
  keeping the same name. Use an H.264 MP4 file without audio for best
  compatibility. Keep a poster image in assets/cinematic-poster.jpg.
  Video is intentionally muted; the optional original ambient music
  is separately controlled by the SOUND ON/OFF button.

PUBLISH & SHARE
  Upload this folder's contents to a static host (Vercel, Netlify,
  Cloudflare Pages, GitHub Pages etc). No build step is required.
  After deployment, the Share button can share/copy the live invitation URL.

NOTES
  This is an invitation concept with fictional-free date and location fields.
  No RSVP backend is implied or implemented.
  Native Web Share availability varies by browser. Link copy fallback is included.
  Designed to support touch screens, desktop, motion preferences and keyboard.
